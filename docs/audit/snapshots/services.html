Assessments

Our comprehensive access technology assessments identify the tools, features, and training strategies that best support each individual’s goals. We evaluate current skills, technology needs, work or educational demands, and accessibility preferences to develop clear, practical recommendations. Each assessment is designed to help clients, families, educators, employers, and rehabilitation professionals make informed decisions about assistive technology, training, and long-term support.

Assistive Technology Training

Our assistive technology training helps individuals build the skills and confidence needed to use accessible technology effectively in everyday life, education, and employment. Training is personalized to each client’s goals and may include screen readers, magnification tools, smartphones, computers, braille displays, productivity software, and other accessibility solutions. Sessions are practical, hands-on, and paced to support lasting independence.

Workplace Accessibility Consulting

Our workplace accessibility consulting services help organizations create more effective, inclusive, and sustainable work environments. We evaluate employee workflows, digital tools, and common workplace tasks to identify practical adjustments that improve visual comfort, efficiency, and performance. Recommendations may include display and contrast settings, text-size modifications, glare reduction, alternative software features, and targeted employee training. Each solution is designed to integrate smoothly into existing systems while supporting productivity, retention, and employee well-being.
